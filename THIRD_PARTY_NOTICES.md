## esu! and osu!

**esu!** is a fan-made rhythm-game theme inside ECHOES, inspired by [osu!](https://osu.ppy.sh/).
It is **not affiliated with or endorsed by ppy Pty Ltd**. “osu!” is a trademark of ppy Pty Ltd.
This repository contains **no osu! assets**: every sprite, sound and font of the built-in look is drawn or
generated by ECHOES itself. osu! beatmaps (`.osz`) and skins (`.osk`) are only read from files the user
already has; they are never redistributed.

## Content

ECHOES can download music, lyrics and videos from public services. Respect the copyright of the
authors and the terms of service of those platforms; download only what you have the right to.
